Pipeline preprocess hien nam o `src/preprocess/pipeline.py`.

Chay thu voi file dang test:

```powershell
.\venv\Scripts\python.exe src\preprocess\pipeline.py --input data\raw\Ngo-doc.pdf
```

Output chinh:

- `data/processed/preprocess_output/<ten-tai-lieu>/chunks.jsonl`
- `data/processed/preprocess_output/<ten-tai-lieu>/manifest.json`
- `data/processed/preprocess_output/<ten-tai-lieu>/markdown_pages/page_XXX.md`
- `data/processed/preprocess_output/<ten-tai-lieu>/tables/page_XXX_table_YY.csv`
- `data/processed/preprocess_output/<ten-tai-lieu>/assets/page_XXX_table_YY.png`
- `data/processed/preprocess_output/<ten-tai-lieu>/assets/page_XXX_visual_YY.png`

Luong xu ly:

1. Parse PDF theo tung trang bang PyMuPDF/pdfplumber.
2. Trich text theo block de giu vi tri, font, thu tu doc.
3. Sua mojibake/loi font pho bien khi UTF-8 bi doc nham cp1252/latin1.
4. Chuan hoa Unicode va ky hieu y khoa/khoa hoc nhu micro, <=, >=, +/-, m^2, H2O.
5. Trich bang bang pdfplumber, luu markdown/CSV/JSON rows/bbox/anh vung bang.
6. Phat hien vung anh/so do/vector drawing, luu bbox va anh crop de phuc hoi khi retrieve.
7. Loai text nam trong bbox bang khoi luong text chinh de bang thanh chunk rieng.
8. Chunk text theo heading/muc/tieu muc truoc, sau do tach tung doan hoac bullet trong moi muc.
9. Gan `semantic_type` cho leaf chunk: symptoms, diagnosis, tests, treatment, complications, hoac general.
10. Ghi JSONL streaming tung chunk, khong giu toan bo tai lieu trong RAM.
